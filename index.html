How it all began

A Dream Rolled
on a d20

The Drunken Dragon was born in 2026 from the dream of four adventurers who believed the world needed a place where fantasy wasn't just a genre — it was a way of life.

Inspired by the taverns of Dungeons & Dragons, the fellowship of The Lord of the Rings, the legendary inns of World of Warcraft, and countless nights of storytelling around a table, they set out to build something real.

A place where every community, every guild, every wandering soul could walk through the door and feel truly welcome. Where the shy wizard finds their voice, the lone ranger finds their party, and the bard always has a stage. No matter who you are or where you come from — at The Drunken Dragon, you belong.

The party

Meet the Founders

Four friends. One dream. An eternal quest to make every night legendary.

Kevin

The Mage

Every great tavern needs a master of the arcane arts — and Kevin is ours. Behind every contract, every deal and every partnership stands his steady hand. He turns the complex into the effortless, keeping the magic alive not just in the glasses, but in everything that makes The Drunken Dragon run.

Hester

The Bard

Hester doesn't just organise events — she crafts experiences. From themed nights to epic celebrations, she has a gift for turning an ordinary evening into a memory you'll be telling stories about for years. If there's a legendary night ahead, Hester planned it.

Luanna

The Druid

Guardian of the tavern's spirit and community. Luanna tends to every soul that walks through the door — and makes sure no one leaves hungry. Every snack is handcrafted by her, made with care to keep every belly full and every heart warm.

Mark

The Ranger

Ever watchful, always prepared. Mark keeps the entire tavern running at full power — every torch lit, every barrel full, every door open. He makes absolutely sure nothing is ever missing and everything is firing on all cylinders before the first guest walks in.
